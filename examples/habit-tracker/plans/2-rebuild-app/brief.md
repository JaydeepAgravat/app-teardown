# Brief: a habit tracker in the space of Loop Habit Tracker

## The product

A small offline habit tracker, informed by what users of the existing app praise and complain about.

## Known

- The starting point is one store link: Loop Habit Tracker on Google Play.
- The user wants it small and offline.
- A first version already exists from the `new-app` run: this folder, `examples/habit-tracker`.

## Not yet known

- Which complaint themes the new app should go after. See `research.md`.
