# Brief: a tiny habit tracker

## The product

One screen that lists the user's daily habits. Tapping a habit marks it done for today. Each habit shows its current streak. The user can add, rename and delete habits.

## Who it is for

One person tracking their own habits on their own phone.

## Known

- No account and no backend.
- It must work with no network.
- History must never be lost.

## Not yet known

- Which platforms, and which stack.
- Whether reminders are in the first version.
- What "never lose my history" has to cover: a force-quit, an app update, a lost phone.
- What counts as "today" when the clock or time zone changes.
- Whether a mistaken tap can be undone, and whether past days can be edited.
