## How it works

Every mobile app is built under the same constraints: an unreliable network, a process the operating system can end at any time, a limited battery, a release that cannot be recalled, and a client the backend cannot trust. Those constraints leave only a few workable designs for each problem, and each design behaves differently in ways you can see.

## What the handbook covers

- **Part I. The teardown method.** The constraints, a model of any app, the probes, and how to turn a signal into a design claim.
- **Part II. Concerns.** One chapter for each aspect of system design, from startup and sync to payments and releases.
- **Part III. Archetypes.** Complete teardowns of app types such as messaging, feeds and ride-hailing, each with notes on how it is asked in a design interview.

The handbook is independent of platform and framework. Logic is shown as neutral pseudocode and diagrams.
