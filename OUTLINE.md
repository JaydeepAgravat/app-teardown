# Outline and plan

**The chapter list is final:** 55 chapters in three parts, plus six appendices. Chapter numbers are fixed.

The list itself lives in one place, [src/data/outline.yaml](src/data/outline.yaml), and the site's Contents page is generated from it.

| Part | Chapters | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| I. The teardown method | 1 to 6 | Constraints, the universal app model, probes, inference, the worksheet |
| II. Concerns | 7 to 38 | Client core, data, media, trust, the device, the business, change over time, quality |
| III. Archetypes | 39 to 55 | Complete teardowns of 17 app types, each with an interview section |
| Appendices | A to F | Probe catalog, worksheet, signal index, glossary, interview guide, further reading |

## Deliberately out of scope

- Build systems, monorepos and continuous integration. They cannot be observed from outside. Chapter 38 summarizes them.
- Platform-specific and framework-specific architecture. The handbook is stack independent.
- Testing strategy. It cannot be observed from outside.

## Two phases

| Phase | Deliverable | Review |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The website with signal lookup, interactive probes, the fillable worksheet, glossary tooltips and reading progress. Chapters 1 to 15 and Chapter 39. | Approve depth, tone, chapter format and site design |
| 2 | Chapters 16 to 38 and 40 to 55. Appendices A, E and F. Full consistency pass, license, hosting. | Final review |

Phase 1 covers each chapter type once: the method, a full run of concern chapters, and one archetype. Phase 2 is about 70% of the writing and follows the format Phase 1 settles.
